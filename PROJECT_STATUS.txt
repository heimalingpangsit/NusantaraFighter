Gameplay/visual pass (2026-09-24)
- Fixed root cause of fighters rendering inside the ULT/S1/S2 button row:
  ground level (GROUND_Y) and the control dock now occupy separate, non-
  overlapping regions of the 320x180 world (see GROUND_Y in Fighter.kt and
  dockTop in GameView.kt).
- Rebuilt the bottom HUD as a single dedicated control dock with a solid
  panel background, plus a new PAUSE button (opens a Resume / Back to Lobby
  overlay).
- True edge-to-edge immersive fullscreen in MainActivity.kt via
  WindowCompat/WindowInsetsControllerCompat (hides status + nav bars, stays
  hidden after a swipe reveal).
- Removed ABAH (had no sprite assets and no working kit) from FighterId,
  CharacterData.kt, and every skill/color branch in GameView.kt.
- Stripped solid-black canvas backgrounds off every character sprite frame
  in assets/characters (FUFU, MR_ETANOL, MR_YOUTUBE, PURBANKYA, RAJA_SOLO)
  via border-flood-fill + thin frame-line cleanup; verified transparent on
  a checkerboard for every state.
- Fighters now always turn to face their opponent (Fighter.faceToward),
  fixing the "facing the wrong way" stiffness.
- Added attack telegraphing: a forward lunge, motion-line/flash VFX during
  the active frames of every attack, and skills/ultimate now put the
  fighter into its attack pose (beginAttackPose) instead of playing no
  animation at all.
- Added a small hit-VFX system (afterAction in GameView.kt): particle
  bursts, floating damage/heal numbers, screen shake, brief hit-stop on
  skill/ultimate hits, and a screen flash on ultimates.
- Idle breathing bob and jump squash/stretch for less static-looking poses.
